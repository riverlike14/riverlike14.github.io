### Financial Mathematics

- [CIR model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cox%E2%80%93Ingersoll%E2%80%93Ross_model) $(dX_t = \kappa(\theta - X_t)dt + \sigma\sqrt{X_t}dW_t)$ does not have a closed-form solution.

### Probability

- If $X \sim N(0, \sigma^2)$, then $\mathbb{E}[X^{2n}] = \sigma^{2n} (2n - 1)!!.$

### Etc

- (Stirling's Formula) $n! \sim \sqrt{2\pi n} \left(\frac{n}{e}\right)^n.$
- (Wallis Product) $\frac{\pi}{2} = \prod_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{4n^2}{4n^2 - 1}.$
